Last Saturday, Anna and her friends went on a trip to the mountains. They left the city early in the morning, before the roads were busy.

The path to the lake was long and steep. After two hours, Anna was tired, but she did not want to stop. "It will be worth it," her friend Dana said.

When they finally arrived, the view was amazing. The water was bright blue, and the air was cold and fresh. The friends took a lot of photos and had a picnic on the grass. Dana brought sandwiches and a big bottle of hot tea.

In the afternoon, the sky became grey. Anna checked the weather forecast on her phone. "Rain is coming," she said. They packed their bags quickly and walked down the mountain.

On the bus home, Anna looked at her photos and smiled. She was tired, but happy. "Next weekend," she thought, "I want to travel somewhere new."
