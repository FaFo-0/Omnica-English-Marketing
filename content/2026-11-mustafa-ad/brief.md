# Mustafa ad: speaking practice, in English

**Status:** script v2, 2026-11-23, waiting for FaFo's OK. Replaces the "founder ad" script (FaFo won't claim to be the founder).
**Goal:** creative for the next creative test (see `.claude/skills/meta-ads-small-budget`). Target: adults who already speak some English and want speaking practice. Destination: same as Ad 1 (WhatsApp messages) so the creatives compare cleanly. Also posted as an organic Reel.
**Format:** vertical, about 20–25 s, Mustafa talking to camera, all in English, slow and clear. Plain background, no recognisable location.
**Mustafa's role:** name only. Not "founder", "owner" or "teacher". Don't imply he's a native speaker.

## Why English only
It filters the audience: people who understand it are the people we want, and the hook turns the video into a little listening test. Captions are **English** (most people watch on mute), big, in brand colors. Russian appears only on the end card.

## Script

| | Spoken (English) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| Hook A | If you can understand me… your English is good enough to start speaking. | caption |
| Hook B | You understand English. But when you have to speak… you freeze? | caption |
| 1 | Hi, I'm Mustafa. | caption + «Omnica English» tag |
| 2 | At Omnica English, you practise speaking one-on-one, with foreign teachers. | caption |
| 3 | And it's not just talking. | caption |
| 4 | After the lesson, you see your mistakes, checked by your teacher and explained in Russian. | screen insert: mistakes card (QA account) |
| 5 | And your new words go straight into your flashcards. | screen insert: flashcards (QA account) |
| 6 | So you get more from every lesson. | caption |
| CTA | Send us a message and book a trial lesson. | end card: «Пробный урок — напишите нам» |

## Recording
- Record **both hooks**. Ad 1 lost two-thirds of viewers in the first 3 seconds, so the hook is the test.
- One line per take, a 1-second pause before and after, and eyes on the lens.
- Also record 5–10 s of silent footage at a laptop to use under the screen inserts.
- Send the clips. Claude edits: cuts, English captions, screen inserts, end card, plus one version per hook.

## Facts to confirm before it runs
- **"Foreign teachers":** keep it only if a new trial student actually gets a teacher from abroad. If not, say "with teachers from different countries".
- **"See your conversation"** (from FaFo's idea) was left out: students see their confirmed mistakes, words, summary and homework, but not the full transcript.
