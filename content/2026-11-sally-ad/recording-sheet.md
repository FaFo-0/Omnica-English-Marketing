# Sally ad — recording sheet

The approved script is unchanged in `sally-motion-test-v1/approved-script.ru.txt`.
This sheet adds a shorter ad cut, alternate hooks and alternate endings, so the edit can test them against each other.
Record **everything below in one session**. Every line is a separate take.

## Before you press record

- Vertical, phone at eye level, lens at eye height. 4K or 1080p, 30 fps. Turn off beauty filters and HDR.
- Face a window (soft light from the front). Don't sit with your back to it.
- Use a clip-on mic if you have one. Otherwise keep the phone within about 1 m and choose a quiet room with soft furniture.
- Turn on airplane mode, so no calls or notifications.
- Before the first take, record **10 seconds of silence** in the room. The editor uses it to clean up the audio.
- Leave space above the head and room at the bottom of the frame for captions and the button.
- Before each line, pause one second and look into the lens. After it, pause again and smile. Small mistakes are fine; just repeat the line.

## A. Hooks — the first 2 seconds (record all 3)

1. «Выучили новое слово на уроке — а через неделю уже не помните?»
2. «Урок английского закончился. А что дальше?»
3. "Your English lesson is just the beginning." *(the approved line, in English)*

## B. Short ad version (~25 seconds) — the main one for paid ads

> В Omnica English вы занимаетесь один на один с преподавателем.
>
> А после урока все новые слова уже ждут вас в личном кабинете — карточки с переводом и примером из вашего урока.
>
> Домашние задания проверяет ваш преподаватель.
>
> Попробуйте — запишитесь на пробный урок.

## C. Full approved script (~50 seconds) — for an organic Reel

Read it exactly as written in `approved-script.ru.txt`. Record each paragraph as a separate take.

## D. Endings (record both)

- For the **paid ad**: «Нажмите на кнопку ниже и запишитесь на пробный урок.»
- For an **organic Reel**: «Ссылка — в профиле.»

## E. Free-library version (optional, a second ad)

> Хотите больше практики на английском — бесплатно?
> Откройте библиотеку Omnica English. Выберите текст, нажмите на незнакомое слово — и сохраните его в свои карточки.
> Библиотека и карточки — бесплатно, даже без занятий.

## F. B-roll (5–10 seconds each, without speaking)

- Sally's hands on the phone: scrolling through flashcards, tapping a word.
- Sally at the laptop in a lesson (Google Meet on screen), nodding and smiling.
- Sally waving at the camera (for the ending).
