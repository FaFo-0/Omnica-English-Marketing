# Ominca English — Initial Brand Usage

## Core asset
`assets/omnica-logo.jpg` is the supplied 2000×2000 logo file.

## What it communicates
- Bright **yellow** field: warmth, optimism, energy, visibility.
- Bold **purple** circular/spiral geometry: progression, focus, systems, learning momentum.
- The mark is distinctive and reads cleanly at small profile-avatar sizes.

## Use now
- Use the complete image, uncropped, as the Instagram, WhatsApp, and Telegram profile image.
- Keep a plain yellow or purple field behind the mark; avoid gradients, busy photos, shadows, and thin outlines.
- Pair it with simple, high-contrast black, white, or purple typography.
- Every Reel should have legible subtitles; do not rely on the logo alone to explain the brand.

## Needed later
- A horizontal wordmark: symbol + **Ominca English** for the website header and presentation covers.
- A one-colour version for print, low-ink documents, and small favicons.
- Social templates for: educational carousel, short lesson Reel cover, platform explanation, and trial offer.

## Naming safety
Use **Ominca English** in new visual text. The owner has confirmed every other variant is a typo. Retain existing social handles for now; only rename them after a deliberate public-account decision.
