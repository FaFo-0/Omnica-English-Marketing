# Ominca English — First 14 Days (No Paid Spend Yet)

## Objective
Turn the existing accounts and logo into a consistent, testable customer path. The brand, core offer, Kazakhstan audience, package prices, and 1,500 ₸ trial are defined; paid advertising remains off until package terms, destination, and conversion tracking are ready.

## Days 1–2 — Make the offer publishable
- Confirm payment/refund terms and initial monthly capacity.
- The verified WhatsApp trial link is ready; add the future public platform sign-up link when it exists.
- FaFo responds to leads as soon as possible; decide a backup person only when lead volume requires it.
- Put logo exports and final brand inputs in the project folder.

**Output:** a publishable offer and a working WhatsApp conversion route.

## Days 3–4 — Complete the public storefront
- Align Instagram, WhatsApp, and Telegram display assets and descriptions.
- Publish/prepare a simple website or landing page for the single CTA.
- Test on mobile from a social link through to a recorded lead and response.

**Output:** a customer can understand the business and take the next step without asking a question.

## Days 5–7 — Seed real value
Create and publish the first six assets, tailored after the audience is confirmed:
1. “Start here”: who Ominca English is for and what it helps with.
2. A practical English correction for a specific real-life situation.
3. A short lesson explaining a common learner mistake.
4. How the learning method works (only verified facts).
5. A scenario-based post: English for a specific workplace/travel/conversation moment.
6. A founder/build-in-public post inviting suitable beta learners to the single CTA.

**Output:** a profile visitors can judge, learn from, and act on.

## Days 8–10 — Learn from humans
- Invite 10–20 people who genuinely resemble the initial target learner; do not spam strangers.
- Ask what they are trying to do in English, what they have tried, why it did not work, and what would make them act now.
- Record exact phrases, objections, and questions in the marketing context.

**Output:** customer language and objections grounded in actual conversations.

## Days 11–14 — Improve the path and choose next test
- Improve bio/landing copy based on real language.
- Review which posts produced saves, shares, profile visits, and conversations—not only views.
- Decide whether the next test is more organic content, a small beta invitation, or a measured ad test.
- If running an ad test later, create 3 distinct creative hypotheses and a fixed budget/review date first.

**Output:** a documented decision backed by observed signal, not guesses.

## Remaining information needed before paid ads or a full website launch
1. What are the payment/refund terms and initial monthly capacity?
2. What is the future public platform sign-up URL?
3. What fixed advertising test budget, date range, and maximum acceptable cost per qualified trial will you approve?
4. When the volume grows, who is the backup lead responder?
