# Ominca English — Instagram Launch System

**Status:** Production-ready draft. Nothing in this folder is published automatically.
**Audience:** Adults in Kazakhstan with Basic–Intermediate English; initial emphasis on work, travel, and relocation.
**Conversion:** One CTA per conversion post: request the 1,500 ₸ trial through the tracked profile link.

## The decision: use your face, but do not make the account depend on it

Ominca sells a human service, not only software. A visitor needs to see that a real person stands behind the lessons, answers messages, and understands the learning problem. Use a **founder-led hybrid**, not a faceless graphics page and not a daily personal vlog.

### Sustainable weekly mix after launch

- **1 face-led Reel:** trust, founder perspective, or a useful English explanation.
- **1 educational carousel:** saveable work/travel English.
- **1 method/offer post:** how Ominca works, package clarity, or a verified platform demonstration.
- **Stories:** 3–5 simple story frames across the week: a poll, the week's phrase, behind-the-scenes work, and one trial reminder.

That makes roughly one-third of feed posts face-led. It is enough to build trust without requiring you to perform on camera every day.

### If speaking Russian on camera feels unnatural

Do not read stiff Russian from a teleprompter. Record naturally in English, then add accurate Russian subtitles and a short Russian headline. The launch conversion path remains Russian. Clear, confident delivery is more trustworthy than forced delivery in the wrong language.

## Recording standard

- Vertical **9:16**, 1080×1920 if possible.
- Keep Reels **20–35 seconds**.
- Face a window; phone at eye level; quiet room; plain background.
- Look into the lens, not at your own face on screen.
- Begin with the problem in the first two seconds—no logo animation or long greeting.
- Record each sentence separately; edit out pauses.
- Add large Russian subtitles inside the middle 70% of the frame.
- End with only one CTA.
- Batch all three launch Reels in one recording session.

## Profile grid and pinning

Pin exactly these three when all are verified and live:

1. **Founder introduction** — who stands behind Ominca.
2. **How it works** — why learning continues between lessons.
3. **Trial and packages** — what it costs and how to start.

Do not create an `Отзывы` Highlight until real, permissioned learner material exists. Empty Highlights look worse than no Highlights.

## Do not do these

- Do not make every post a polished advertisement.
- Do not use stock photos of fake students or invent testimonials.
- Do not promise fluency, results, scores, or employment outcomes.
- Do not publish private dashboard/student information in screen recordings.
- Do not buy followers or automate unsolicited DMs.
- Do not wait for perfect equipment; clear sound and useful content matter more.
