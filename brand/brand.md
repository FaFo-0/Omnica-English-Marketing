# Omnica English — brand

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Purple | `#6716A4` | Main brand color: backgrounds and headlines on light backgrounds |
| Yellow | `#FFCA00` | Accent: one highlighted word, price, buttons, curves. Use it sparingly |
| Cream | `#FFF9ED` | Light background (instead of pure white) |
| Ink | `#33253C` | Body text on light backgrounds |
| Lavender | `#EBDDF3` | Soft panels and chat bubbles on cream |
| Deep purple | `#391052` | Shadows and dark accents on purple |

Combinations that work: white text on purple, purple on cream, purple on yellow. Never yellow text on cream or white, because it can't be read.

## Fonts

- **Inter** (the same font as the app) for everything: headlines Bold/ExtraBold, body Regular/Medium.
- **PT Serif Italic** for **one** accent word or phrase per design (the «продолжается.» look). It's free and fully supports Cyrillic.
- On a 1080 px canvas: headline at least 80 px, body at least 40 px. If it isn't readable on a phone held at arm's length, it's too small.

Earlier work (the intro carousel) used Arial and Georgia Italic. New work uses Inter and PT Serif.

## Logo

Files live in `public/brand/tenant/` in the app repo (FaFo-0/omnica-english); don't copy them here.
- `logo.svg`: round mark (avatar, small corners)
- `lockup-light.svg` / `lockup-dark.svg`: mark plus wordmark (on light / dark backgrounds)
- `logo-white.svg`, `logo-yellow.svg`: single-color versions

Keep clear space around the logo of at least half the mark's height. Don't stretch it, recolor it or add effects.

## Layout (Instagram)

- Story / Reel: 1080 × 1920. Keep text inside **y 250–1650** (Instagram covers the top and bottom).
- Feed post / carousel: 1080 × 1350. **Leave the top-right corner empty**, because Instagram's "1/7" counter covers it.
- **One idea per Story.** A Story gets about 3 seconds: one headline, at most one short line under it, one picture.
- Screenshots: phone-sized, cropped to the part that matters, large enough to read.

## Voice

- Russian with «вы». Short sentences, spoken style, the way Mustafa would say it.
- Concrete over clever: "Слова с урока — в ваших карточках", not "Умная система запоминания".
- **No hype words:** умный, уникальный, революционный, лучший, «в вашу память», «так не бывает».
- Say **уроки / занятия**, never "баллы" / points.
- Don't promise results we can't show. No fake reviews; real reviews only with permission.
- Emoji: at most one or two per caption, none in the designs.

## Facts we repeat (check against POLICY.md before publishing)

- Online lessons, one-to-one with a teacher, on Google Meet.
- After a lesson: the lesson's words appear in the student's flashcards with translation and an example, and the teacher checks the homework.
- The library and flashcards are free, with no lessons to buy. Registration is needed.
- Trial lesson: a normal full lesson with the teacher.
- Teachers: native speakers and Russian-speaking teachers, from different countries.
- **Never put prices or ₸ in designs, bios or captions.** Students come from all over Central Asia and prices depend on their country. Say «пробный урок» and send people to omnicaenglish.com/prices.
- Audience: Russian speakers across Central Asia. Never imply Kazakhstan only.
